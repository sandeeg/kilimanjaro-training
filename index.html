Garmin

Import each person's Garmin activities and the kilometres and climbing fill themselves in. Everything is parsed in your own browser — no Garmin login, nothing uploaded, nothing stored outside this device.

Getting the file

  1. Open Garmin Connect → Activities on a computer.
  2. Scroll far enough back to load every hike you want — the export only contains what's currently on screen.
  3. Click Export CSV (top right). A Activities.csv downloads.
  4. Pick the matching units below, then choose that file.

Activities are matched to a training weekend by week, not exact day, so it doesn't matter which day you actually went. On-foot activities only — rides and swims are ignored. Nothing is written until you press Apply.
